Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Curry and Bevans Ltd. (company no. 05643474) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we use personal information when you contact us or use this website.

Information we collect

When you email or call us, we collect the details you choose to share, such as your name, company, contact details and information about your project.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide quotations
  • To deliver and support the work you've asked us to carry out
  • To meet our legal and contractual obligations

We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project records only for as long as we need them for the purposes above, or as required by law.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at info@curryandbevans.com. You can also raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

This website

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps. Google may record your IP address and set its own cookies when that map loads.
