About us

Family-run since 1986. Engineering-led ever since.

For nearly four decades, we've delivered reliable, forward-thinking electrical solutions backed by a dedicated team of specialist engineers.

Our story

From family firm to trusted engineering partner

Curry and Bevans Ltd. was founded in 1986 as a family-orientated electrical engineering business. Since then we've grown into a name clients across the UK rely on, from new build installations to bespoke automation systems.

That growth came from reputation. We've kept the same family values throughout: do the job right, be straight with people, and be there when you need us.

Our commitment

We believe in doing things properly. High-quality workmanship, forward-thinking engineering, and a service built on honesty and professionalism.
